# VROOQ — Product Marketing Context (working draft)

**Document version:** v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-21  
**Status:** Extracted from current repository; founder validation pending.

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** VROOQ gives cars a lasting digital garage and connects people around their automotive interests.

**What it does:** Users can create a garage, document a vehicle's history, publish content, browse communities and convoy concepts, and explore a marketplace. Some experiences remain local or simulated in the current codebase.

**Category:** Automotive social platform / digital vehicle profile.
**Product type:** Consumer community with marketplace experiments.
**Business model:** Undecided. Existing payment function is a mock; no proven live revenue.

## Target Audience
**Hypothesis:** Enthusiasts who build, restore, maintain, collect, and share cars.
**Primary job:** Showcase a car and its story, discover similar owners and practical knowledge.
**Other jobs:** Track ownership/maintenance evidence; find local people/events; explore relevant listings.

**Geography:** Not confirmed (repo uses English, USD and examples from Europe; `vrooq.ma` suggests Morocco, but a launch-market decision is required).

## Problems and Risks
- Car histories are fragmented across photos, conversations and service receipts.
- Mainstream social feeds rarely make the vehicle the enduring organizing entity.
- The product cannot claim verified ownership, records, transactions, or network traction until backed by actual validation and reliable data.

## Differentiation Hypothesis
**Vehicle-first profile:** A persistent automotive identity with service history and social activity associated with the car rather than an ephemeral post. Validate whether users actually value this compared with existing communities.

## Positioning / Voice
**Current design direction:** Dark, technical, performance-inspired, restrained.
**Copy rules:** Plain, specific, energetic; avoid 'most exclusive', 'verified' or 'trusted' unless supported by evidence. No fabricated testimonials or metrics.
**Primary CTA hypothesis:** Create your garage.
**Activation milestone proposal:** First real vehicle added with one genuine photo.

## Competitors
To research and validate: Instagram/TikTok car accounts (indirect), owner forums/communities, vehicle-history tools, niche car-social apps.

## Goals
1. Build an honest, stable first-session experience.
2. Measure signup completion, first vehicle added and first useful interaction.
3. Develop retention before expanding paid features.

## Decisions to Resolve
- Launch geography and language(s).
- Focus of the initial audience: tuners, collectors, everyday owners, or clubs.
- Core experience at launch: social feed vs persistent garage/history.
- Marketplace scope and seller verification.
- Whether an explicitly labeled demo mode is retained.
- Monetization only after trust and product-market fit.
